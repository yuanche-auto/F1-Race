## Reading

* **H-G and H-D hold** for the two controllers they compare: identification from one exploration
  lap plus re-planning carries over to new tracks (100 / 100) and to wet friction (50 / 50, from
  exploration laps that ended after ~460 m); the Spielberg-trained policy does not (0 / 100, 8 / 50).
  The two controllers tied for first on Spielberg's dry cars are now at the two ends of the ranking.
* **RL as a method** (the unselected distribution): every one of the 22 trained policies – the nine
  L5 runs, the ten L7 runs of plain training and of the failure-start curriculum, the selected ones
  – loses almost all completion on the new tracks and in the wet (L5 finals: p = 0.0039 for each).
  Training varied the car's parameters, the steering lag and the L3 disturbances, never the track
  and never friction below 0.92. **Interpretation, not tested in L8:** the residual corrections are
  specific to Spielberg's corners – the test tracks' maximum plan curvature lies within Spielberg's
  range, though their corner sequences, widths and speed–curvature combinations were not examined;
  training on several tracks would be the test – work after L8.
* The L3 ranking survives the new tracks: under the disturbances the SMPC, with Spielberg's
  back-offs transferred unchanged, is the most reliable (33 / 40). Its back-offs transfer without
  recalibration at +3.9 s per lap.
* Short fine-tuning on the target (~60 laps of other cars) is not a substitute for identification
  here: it is seed-dependent (0–34 of 35), fails on one track entirely and makes the wet worse.
* For L9: the identification controllers rest on a re-plan (~1 min, ~7.4 GB) per car and condition,
  computed offline here; under a real-time budget that cost is part of the comparison.

**Not verified / limits:** the second environment (protocol section 9; L7 A10) – H-G and H-D are not
cross-checked; four test tracks after one replacement (selection effect, above); wet is a friction
factor only; the Spielberg reference mixes L7's 120 s and L8's 121 s lap limits (no effect on
completed laps); the mechanisms marked as interpretation were not tested.
